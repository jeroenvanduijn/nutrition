Common questions

Everything you need to know

Do I need to track calories?

It depends on what works for you. Some people love data and find tracking helpful. Others prefer simple principles like "protein with every meal" and "fill half your plate with vegetables". We adapt to your preference.

Is this very restrictive?

No. We focus on what you should eat more of, not what you cannot have. The goal is to feel good and build sustainable habits, not to follow strict rules. We only add restrictions if you specifically want more structure.

Can I join if I am vegetarian or have allergies?

Yes. Both programs work with all diets and preferences. We build your plan around what you eat and what you enjoy. Just let us know your needs during the starting call.

Can I repeat the 28-Day Kickstart?

Absolutely. Many members do the Kickstart a few times throughout the year to reset their habits or try a new focus area. It is a great way to stay on track.

Do I need to be a CrossFit Leiden member?

No. These nutrition programs are open to everyone in Leiden and beyond. You do not need to be a gym member to join.

How do I get started?

Click one of the buttons on this page to choose your program. Then email us at nutrition@crossfitleiden.nl and we will set up your starting call. Easy.

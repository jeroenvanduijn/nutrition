Common questions

Do I need to track calories?

Not unless you want to. We can use numbers if they help you, or simple principles if your life is already busy.

Is this restrictive?

No. We avoid extreme rules. We focus on healthy habits you can keep up.

Can I repeat the Kickstart?

Yes, once. After that we recommend the 1-on-1 coaching program.

Do I need to be a CrossFit Leiden member?

No. The Kickstart and the 1-on-1 coaching are open to everyone.

How do I start?

Book your Goal Review. We help you choose the best option.
